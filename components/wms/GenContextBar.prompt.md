One-line: keeps the GEN and its key details visible for the whole task, below the stepper.

```jsx
<GenContextBar gen="GEN-2025-05-20-0012" items={[
  {label:'Date & Time of Receipt',value:'12 Aug 2026, 09:40'},
  {label:'Supplier',value:'ABC Supplies Pvt. Ltd.'},
  {label:'Driver',value:'Ramesh Yadav · 98xxx xxxxx'}
]} />
```

Appears only after the GEN has been generated. The number is always mono — it gets read aloud, written on a slip and typed back in.

One-line: the two-button bar at the foot of a handheld screen.

```jsx
<ActionBar>
  <Button variant="quiet" block>Cancel</Button>
  <Button variant="accent" block>Next</Button>
</ActionBar>
```

Both buttons take `block` so they split the width evenly. The forward action is soft teal (`accent`), not deep teal — deep teal is reserved for the marketing site's primary action.
